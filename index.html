Growth product case study

Understanding SIP retention friction

Exploring what public customer feedback can—and cannot—tell us about the experience of first-time mutual fund investors on Groww.

North-star outcome: Increase first-time SIP investors with an active SIP 12 months after their first SIP. Active = at least one successful instalment in the last 30 days.
Evidence integrity: This prototype uses keyword-assisted screening of 110 public reviews, not a trained AI model. Labels are preliminary and require human validation. Public reviews do not prove first-time investor status, actual SIP discontinuation, or the cause of churn.

2. Opportunity hypotheses to validate

These are research hypotheses—not confirmed product requirements or proven causes.

Make SIP schedule actions predictable

Show the exact pause/skip eligibility window, cutoff time, and confirmation state. Validate whether users understand the rules and whether failures are occurring.

Clarify plan type and charges before investing

Explain direct vs regular plan selection and applicable charges in plain language before confirmation. Validate comprehension and support contacts.

Hypothesis: transparency
Set clear expectations for redemption and switching

Show expected processing stages and status updates. Validate whether uncertainty affects investor confidence.

4. AI evidence log & limitations

  • Input: 110 public Groww reviews from Google Play and Trustpilot, supplied for this project.
  • Method: keyword-assisted classification into tentative product category, feedback theme, explicit retention signal, and SIP relevance.
  • Human review: all rows remain marked “Pending human review”.
  • Not inferred: age, income, investor tenure, first-time investor status, SIP cancellation, or causal relationship to retention.
  • Bias: public reviewers are self-selected; the dataset contains substantial stock/trading feedback and may not represent SIP investors.
  • Next validation: conduct interviews or a survey with first-time SIP investors, including active, paused, missed-instalment, and discontinued users.
Prepared as a product-discovery prototype. This is not investment advice and does not recommend any mutual fund or security. Review classifications are indicative and should be manually checked against the original text.
